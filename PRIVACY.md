# Privacy notice: St. Pete AI Safety Browser

The Safety Browser is built to protect you, not to watch you. It has no accounts, no ads, no tracking and no analytics.

## What stays on your computer

Everything you do in the browser stays on your computer: the pages you visit, your Favorites, History and Notes, and anything you type. St. Pete AI never receives any of it.

The scam checks (look-alike addresses, fake virus warnings, payment pressure, the password guard and the download guard) all run on your computer.

## The only outside connections the browser makes

| Service | What is sent | Why | How often |
|---|---|---|---|
| **openphish.com** | A request for the list. Nothing about you or your browsing. | To download the list of known phishing sites, which is then checked on your computer. | About every 6 hours |
| **rdap.org** | The **name of each website** you visit (for example `example.com`), but not the full page address. | To find out how old a website is. Brand-new websites are a common sign of a scam. | Once per website |
| **github.com** | A request for the latest version number. | To download security and safety updates automatically. | At startup and every 6 hours |

Websites you visit see your visit as they would in any browser.

## Your data

Your browser data is stored in `%APPDATA%\St. Pete AI Safety Browser` on your computer. Uninstalling the program does not delete it, so you can reinstall without losing your Favorites. Delete that folder to remove it completely.
